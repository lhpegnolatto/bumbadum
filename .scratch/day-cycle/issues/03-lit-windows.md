# 03 — Lit windows on buildings and the food truck

**What to build:** `building-cafe`, `building-brick`, `building-radio`, `building-records` and `food-truck` gain a window mask: an image the size of the tile holding only its lit window pixels, declared through the tile's `windows` property (ADR-0029). The generator packs the masks in the atlas and attaches each mask's frame to the object's light source. The engine draws them in the light layer, outside the Ambient light, switched on and off like any other light source.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Occlusion:** the light layer sits above every Player and Car, and the tiles in front of buildings are walkable. Each window mask is cut by an inverse mask of the silhouettes of the Players and Cars drawn in front of its object (greater depth), rebuilt every frame, so a lit storefront never covers an Avatar on the sidewalk.

- [ ] Window masks are generated by a script from the object images and reviewed by a human.
- [ ] The generator fails on a window mask whose size differs from its tile.
- [ ] Windows come on and off one by one with the Lamp glow, and keep their colour while the plaza darkens.
- [ ] A Player or Car in front of a lit window is not covered by it.
- [ ] `pnpm check` is green.
