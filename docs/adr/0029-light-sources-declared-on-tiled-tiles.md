# Light sources are declared on Tiled tiles

Lamp glow follows the Time of day and each light switches on at its own moment, so a single pre-rendered light image can no longer carry it. Instead, a tile in the Tiled objects tileset declares the light it sheds: a `glow` kind (such as `warm` or `stage`) with an offset to its bulb, and optionally a `windows` mask holding only its lit window pixels. The generator exports one light source per placed object, and the engine draws each glow and window mask in a light layer above the world, outside the Ambient light, so lit windows and lamps keep their colour while the rest of the Map darkens. This keeps ADR-0016's rule that Tiled is the canonical map source.

## Considered options

- Keep the pre-rendered light image and fade it as a whole: every light would brighten in unison and moving a lamp would still leave its glow behind.
- A separate Tiled point layer with one point per light: 35 lamps placed twice, free to drift away from the lamp they belong to.
- Swap each building for a night variant above a threshold: the night sprite sits under the Ambient light, so its yellow windows are darkened with everything else and change abruptly.

## Consequences

- Moving or adding a lamp in Tiled moves or adds its glow; no light image is regenerated by hand.
- The Lamp glow level is the share of light sources switched on, each with a fixed threshold derived from its position, so lights come on one by one at dusk.
- The light layer ignores depth order, so window masks are cut by the silhouettes of the Players and Cars in front of their object; a glow is soft enough to fall over them.
